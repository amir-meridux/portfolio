# Featured Engineering Projects

## 1. Meridux PatientCore

### AI-Powered Patient Workflow Automation Platform

### Business Challenge

Healthcare providers frequently rely on fragmented manual processes for appointment requests, patient communication, duplicate checking, reporting, and follow-up activities. These disconnected workflows increase administrative effort, reduce operational visibility, and introduce avoidable errors.

### Engineering Solution

Meridux PatientCore is an end-to-end workflow automation platform that transforms patient enquiries into structured operational workflows through intelligent routing, workflow orchestration, business rules, and automated communications.

### Engineering Highlights

- End-to-end workflow orchestration
- Intelligent patient routing
- Duplicate & race-condition prevention
- Modular workflow architecture
- Automated confirmation emails
- Operational reporting
- Structured audit logging
- Error handling & validation

### Technology Stack

- n8n
- JavaScript
- REST APIs
- Google Workspace
- Gmail API
- Google Sheets
- OpenAI

## 2. Prospect Intelligence Engine (PIE)

### AI-Powered Business Discovery & Prospect Intelligence Platform

### Business Challenge

Sales teams often rely on fragmented manual research to discover businesses, evaluate prospects, collect contact information, and prepare outreach activities. This limits scalability and reduces data consistency.

### Engineering Solution

Prospect Intelligence Engine is a modular automation platform that supports business discovery, website intelligence, API governance, contact enrichment, validation, and scalable prospect processing.

Every system I build follows a consistent engineering methodology.

- Preserve data integrity through validation and workflow governance.
- Build modular architectures that simplify future expansion.
- Prioritise operational visibility through monitoring and reporting.
- Apply AI where it produces measurable operational value.
